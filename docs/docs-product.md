# Tribe — Product Definition

> Working name. The consumer-facing brand may change before public launch.

## Product Thesis

Social media has increasingly shifted from maintaining relationships toward consuming content.

Tribe explores a different model:

**A private social network for the people you actually know.**

The network is intentionally finite. Each person may have a maximum of 150 reciprocal connections.

The product should optimize for meaningful interaction between people, not content consumption, reach, influence, or time spent in the application.

## Problem

Existing social platforms create several incentives that conflict with maintaining real relationships:

- follower accumulation
- audience building
- algorithmic feeds
- infinite content consumption
- public engagement metrics
- influencer and creator dynamics
- viral distribution
- advertising-driven engagement optimization

Meanwhile, communication with actual friends and family is fragmented across:

- group texts
- iMessage
- WhatsApp
- Instagram Close Friends
- private Instagram accounts
- shared photo albums
- family group chats

There is an opportunity for a dedicated social space centered around a finite set of real relationships.

## Product Promise

**Keep up with the people you actually care about.**

Tribe provides:

- reciprocal relationships rather than followers
- a finite social graph
- private sharing
- chronological updates
- text and photos
- a feed that ends
- no influence mechanics

## Core Product Principles

### 1. Relationships are reciprocal

There is no follower/following model.

Two users are either connected or they are not.

### 2. The network is finite

Each user may have a maximum of:

**150 accepted connections**

This is a hard product constraint.

It is not a paid limit and should not become one.

### 3. Private by default

Content should only be visible to authorized users.

For the MVP, posts are visible only to:

- the author
- accepted connections of the author

Privacy rules must be enforced by the backend, not merely hidden in the UI.

### 4. Chronological over algorithmic

The feed is ordered by time.

The MVP must not rank posts according to:

- engagement
- predicted interest
- popularity
- virality
- advertising value
- recommendation models

### 5. The feed ends

Users should be able to consume everything their network has shared and reach an explicit end state.

Example:

> You're caught up.  
> That's everything your people have shared.

The application should not manufacture additional content merely to keep the user engaged.

### 6. People over audiences

The product should not expose mechanics that incentivize users to accumulate status.

Avoid:

- follower counts
- view counts
- share counts
- public popularity metrics
- trending content
- public leaderboards

### 7. Technology should strengthen offline relationships

Long term, Tribe should be judged by whether it improves relationships between people.

Time spent in the application is not inherently a positive metric.

A successful interaction may result in someone closing Tribe and contacting another person elsewhere.

## Core User Loop

1. Join Tribe.
2. Create a profile.
3. Connect with people you know.
4. Share an update.
5. See updates from your connections.
6. Interact with those updates.
7. Return when your people have something new to share.

## MVP Target User

The initial user is someone who:

- has close friends or family they want to keep up with
- shares personal updates selectively
- dislikes some aspects of mainstream social media
- does not need or want a public audience
- would prefer a quieter social experience

The MVP does not need to serve every demographic.

## Initial Distribution Model

The MVP should grow primarily through direct invitations.

Example:

Dakota invites Sarah.

Sarah joins and accepts the connection.

Sarah later invites Mike without being prompted by Dakota.

That second invitation is an important product signal.

## Product Language

Prefer relationship-oriented language.

Preferred:

- Your people
- Connections
- Your Tribe
- Invite
- You're caught up

Avoid:

- Followers
- Audience
- Reach
- Impressions
- Growth score
- Influencer
- Creator metrics

## Visual Personality

The product should feel:

- calm
- human
- private
- warm
- trustworthy
- simple
- premium
- understated

It should not feel:

- gamified
- hyperactive
- viral
- content-maximizing
- creator-oriented
- ad-driven

Photography and personal updates should be the primary visual content.

## Long-Term Hypotheses

These ideas may be explored after the MVP is validated.

They are not currently approved features.

### Relationship layers

Potential Dunbar-inspired layers:

- inner circle
- close relationships
- friends
- wider community

### Relationship maintenance

Potential tools:

- birthdays
- shared memories
- lightweight reconnect reminders
- invitations to spend time together
- relationship activity history

### Private groups or circles

Potential use cases:

- family
- close friends
- trips
- life events

These should only be pursued if observed user behavior supports them.

## Product Test

The first question Tribe must answer is not:

> Do people like the idea?

It is:

> Will groups of real-life friends or family voluntarily use a finite private social network repeatedly?

That is the primary purpose of the MVP.
